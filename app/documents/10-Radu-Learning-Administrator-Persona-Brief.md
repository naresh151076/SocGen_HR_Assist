### Suggested starts (New chat)

- Check a group for an approved session

- Investigate invitation status  

### Navigation (Radu only)

| Destination | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **New chat / Assistant** | Turn an approved plan into registrations and invitations with decisions only |
| **Operations** | Package inbox: NMF registration check, publish draft, Thomas hold, supporting packages |
| **Session readiness** | Cohort pipeline, donuts, exception callouts, Amélie → Claire → Elena strip |
| **Help** | Profile menu only — numbered ops path without competing with queues |

Radu does **not** own Claire’s unit dashboard, Amélie’s programme design as primary work, or Elena’s control catalogue as his home.

### Ownership boundary

| Owner | Owns |
|---|---|
| **Claire** | Business need and trade-offs (Priya alternative, Thomas later session) |
| **Amélie** | Feasible published plan |
| **Radu** | Registration rules application, register/invite after approval, operational evidence |
| **Elena** | Control exceptions and remediation proof |

**Product promise:** One operational intent; routine checks in the background; Radu handles only decisions and approvals.  
**Governance line:** Do not register anyone or send invitations without Radu’s approval.

### What Radu never does

Become the business owner of who must attend · redesign the curriculum · close INV-04 as Control Lead · treat intake files / MyLearning / Outlook as separate primary destinations.

## 3. Screen journey for presenters

Use screenshots in this order.

### Slide A — New chat

**Title:** One operational intent, not five systems  

Radu lands on: “What learning operation needs your decision?” He describes the registration or invitation work in language. Intake files, MyLearning, HR data and Outlook stay as evidence — not left-rail destinations.

**Say:** The product brings answers to one desk and only asks when a choice matters.

### Slide B — Conversation + check outcome

**Title:** Ten ready, two decisions — clear in five seconds  

Group check completes with Ready / Needs decision / Capacity. Exception cards explain Priya and Thomas with recommendations. Radu can prepare the package but does not register or invite until he approves.

**Say:** Radu is the decision-maker; AI is not the hero.

### Slide C — Operations

**Title:** Package inbox for registration work  

Master-detail operations board: NMF registration check, publish draft, Thomas hold, supporting packages. Status and coverage replace spreadsheet queues. CTAs reopen the Assistant with the connected ops scenario.

**Say:** Work arrives as packages with owners, not as raw CSV dumps.

### Slide D — Session readiness

**Title:** Pipeline and handoffs at a glance  

Cohort pipeline, readiness donuts, exception callouts, and Amélie → Claire → Elena strip (including INV-04 awareness). Oversight board; consequential approve/send returns to chat.

**Say:** Readiness is visual; publish stays governed.

## 4. What’s special in this conversational AI approach

1. **One intent → consolidated checks** — eligibility, duplicates, calendar, capacity without tool-hopping.  
2. **Human approval for consequential steps** — register and invite only after Radu confirms.  
3. **Exceptions as decisions** — Priya/Thomas are explainable cards, not buried rule codes.  
4. **Business handback** — Claire owns trade-offs; Radu does not invent business policy.  
5. **Operations + Session readiness** — queue and pipeline without an Excel control room.  
6. **Audit trail language** — what was checked, result, rule basis, evidence reference, next action.

## 7. Closing line

For Radu, conversational AI is not “auto-register everyone.” It is **one operational intent, background checks, human approval, and a clear audit trail** — with business exceptions returned to Claire and invitation proof ready for Elena.
